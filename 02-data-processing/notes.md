# Module 2 - Data processing

## Legal bases (art. 7) - you need at least one to process personal data
1. consent
2. legal or regulatory obligation
3. public administration (public policies)
4. research bodies (anonymize when possible)
5. contract execution or preliminary steps, at the data subject's request
6. exercise of rights in legal/administrative processes
7. protection of life or physical safety
8. health protection (health professionals/services)
9. legitimate interest of the controller (not above the person's rights)
10. credit protection

Consent must be free, informed and for a specific purpose. Can be revoked any time.

## Sensitive data (art. 11)
- stricter: consent must be specific and highlighted
- without consent only in specific cases (legal obligation, health, life protection,
  fraud prevention, research...)
- legitimate interest and credit protection are NOT valid for sensitive data

## Children and teenagers (art. 14)
- always in their best interest
- children (under 12): specific consent from at least one parent/guardian
- games and apps can't ask for more data than needed
- ECA Digital (Lei 15.211/2025) added more rules and the ANPD enforces it
- the course also covers elderly people: clear, simple information for them

## Sharing and international transfer
- sharing needs a legal basis and the data subject should know about it
- international transfer (art. 33) only to countries with adequate protection,
  with standard contract clauses, specific consent, or the other cases in the law

## End of processing (art. 15-16)
data must be deleted when the purpose ends, except legal obligation, research
(anonymized), transfer allowed by law, or exclusive use by the controller (anonymized)
