# Module 3 - Security and privacy

## Privacy
- privacy by design: think about data protection from the start of the project
- privacy by default: the default settings are the most private ones
- collect only what you need (necessity principle)

## Security (art. 46-49)
- controllers and operators must use technical and administrative measures to protect data
  from unauthorized access, loss, leaks, etc.
- security incident that can cause relevant risk -> tell the ANPD and the data subjects
  (ANPD regulation: 3 business days)
- examples: access control, encryption, logs, backups, pseudonymization, training people

## Penalties (art. 52)
- warning
- simple fine: up to 2% of the company's revenue in Brazil, max R$ 50 million **per infraction**
- daily fine (same 50 million cap)
- making the infraction public
- blocking or deleting the personal data involved
- partial suspension of the database or of the processing activity, full ban of processing
- public bodies don't get fines but get the other sanctions

## LGPD and other laws
- Constitution: data protection is a fundamental right (EC 115/2022)
- Marco Civil da Internet (Lei 12.965/2014)
- Lei de Acesso à Informação (Lei 12.527/2011) - LAI and LGPD work together in the public sector
- Código de Defesa do Consumidor
- inspired by the European GDPR
