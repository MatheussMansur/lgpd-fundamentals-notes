# Module 4 - Putting LGPD in place

This module uses Serpro (the federal data processing company) as the example.

## How a company/agency usually adapts
1. **mapping**: find every place personal data is collected, stored and shared (data inventory)
2. **diagnosis / gap analysis**: compare what exists with what the law asks
3. **plan**: decide what to change, who is responsible, deadlines
4. **implementation**: policies, privacy notices, contracts with operators, security measures,
   process to answer data subjects
5. **monitoring**: audits, training, update the inventory, incident response

## Documents that show up a lot
- data inventory / records of processing
- privacy policy and privacy notice
- RIPD (relatório de impacto à proteção de dados) for high risk processing
- incident response plan

## Answering data subjects (art. 19)
- confirmation of existence / access: simplified format right away, or a complete
  statement within **15 days**
- the request is free of charge
